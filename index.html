Privacy Policy

Contacto - Smart Call Tracker

Last updated: December 20, 2025

Your privacy is our priority. Contacto is designed with a privacy-first approach. All your data stays on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

Overview

Contacto ("we", "our", or "the app") is a call tracking and contact management application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Access

To provide our services, Contacto requires access to:

  • Call Logs: We read your device's call history to display call statistics, track call patterns, and provide insights about your calling habits.
  • Contacts: We access your contacts to display caller names, allow you to tag and organize contacts, and provide contact-related features.
  • Notifications: We may send local notifications to remind you about contacts you haven't called in a while.

How Your Data Is Stored

100% Local Storage: All data processed by Contacto is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have servers that collect, store, or process your personal information.
  • Call statistics and history remain on your device
  • Contact tags and notes are stored locally
  • App preferences and settings are saved on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers

Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we don't do:

  • We do NOT upload your call logs to any server
  • We do NOT share your contacts with third parties
  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT sell any personal information
  • We do NOT use analytics that identify individual users

Third-Party Services

Contacto may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: Required for app distribution and updates on Android devices.
  • Crash Reporting: Anonymous crash reports may be collected to help us improve app stability. These reports do not contain personal information.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security measures. We recommend:

  • Using a screen lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face recognition)
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated
  • Being cautious about which apps you install

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app removes all associated data
  • Permissions: You can revoke app permissions at any time through your device settings

Children's Privacy

Contacto is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
